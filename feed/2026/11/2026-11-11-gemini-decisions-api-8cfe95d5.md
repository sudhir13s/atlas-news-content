## Summary

Google has entered the decision model category with the release of Gemini Decision 1. The model API supports yes/no questions, choices, and rubric-scoring, and is priced at 10 cents per million input tokens with free output. Unlike Jev and OpenAI's equivalents, Gemini Decision 1 is fully multi-modal and handles text, images, audio, video, and PDFs.
