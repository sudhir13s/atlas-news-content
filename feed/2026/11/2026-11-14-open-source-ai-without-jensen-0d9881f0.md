## Summary

The resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang creates significant challenges for the US open model ecosystem, as his funding and championing of open models are expected to dry up. Open-source models face severe hurdles, including compute shortages, lack of top-tier talent concentration compared to closed labs, and impending risks of strict state and national regulation. The ecosystem requires a major incubator or new coalition of allies among hyperscalers to sustain long-term innovation against dominant closed-source labs.

## Why It Matters

The departure of a key industry champion threatens the funding, regulatory protection, and strategic momentum supporting open-source AI models in the United States.
